Plain-Language Summary

CWI AI LLC operates cwiai.net and provides AI-powered receptionist services. We collect information you give us voluntarily (inquiry forms, phone conversations, demo requests) and standard technical data your browser sends automatically. We use that information to provide our service, contact you, and improve our platform. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

1Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways when you visit our website, contact us, or use our services:

Information you provide directly:

  • Name, email address, phone number, and business details submitted through contact forms, demo request forms, or scheduling tools.
  • Messages, questions, and inquiries submitted through chat widgets or email.
  • Payment information processed when you subscribe to a paid plan (handled by our payment processor — see Section 5).

Information from AI-assisted interactions:

  • Transcripts or summaries of conversations conducted through our AI chat or voice receptionist features, where applicable.
  • Caller-provided information (name, callback number, nature of inquiry) captured during AI-handled phone interactions on behalf of our clients.

Cookies and tracking: We use first-party cookies for essential site function (session management) and may use third-party analytics cookies to understand aggregate traffic patterns. 3AI and Automated Processing Disclosure

Important Notice Regarding AI Processing

CWI AI's core service involves artificial intelligence systems that process your inquiries, calls, and messages in an automated manner. By submitting an inquiry through our chat widget, contact form, or phone system, your message may be received, interpreted, and responded to by an AI model before or instead of a human agent.

Specifically, the following AI-assisted processes may occur when you interact with CWI AI or any of our clients' AI receptionists:

  • Natural language processing (NLP). Your written or spoken inquiry is analyzed by a language model to determine intent, extract relevant details (such as requested services, preferred appointment times, or callback numbers), and generate an appropriate response.
  • Automated routing. Based on the content of your inquiry, the AI may route your request to a specific calendar, department, or escalation queue without human review.
  • Conversation logging. AI-handled conversations may be stored as transcripts to enable follow-up by a human team member, for quality assurance purposes, and for ongoing model improvement (in aggregate, de-identified form where possible).
  • Voice recognition. For voice-based interactions, your spoken words may be transcribed and processed by automated speech-recognition systems.

CWI AI uses third-party AI model providers (including OpenRouter and its upstream model providers) to power these capabilities. Data transmitted to those providers is subject to their respective data processing agreements, which include confidentiality and data security obligations.

You are not required to interact with our AI systems. If you prefer to speak directly with a human, you may reach us at (734) 812-9971 or email brian@cwiai.net.

4Data Storage and Security

CWI AI LLC stores data using reputable cloud infrastructure and SaaS platforms. Data may be stored and processed in the United States. We implement the following security practices:

  • Encryption of data in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher for all web communications.
  • Access controls limiting data access to authorized personnel who require it to perform their job functions.
  • Regular review of third-party vendor security practices and data processing agreements.
  • Prompt incident response procedures in the event of a suspected breach or unauthorized access.

No method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your personal information using commercially reasonable means, we cannot guarantee absolute security. In the event of a data breach that affects your personal information, we will notify you as required by applicable law.

Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services and fulfill the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law. When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it in a reasonably secure manner. You may request deletion of your data at any time — see Section 6 for your rights.

6Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the following rights with respect to your personal information. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information in Section 7.

  • Right to access. You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to correction. You may request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete information we hold about you.
  • Right to deletion. You may request that we delete your personal information. We will honor this request unless we are required to retain the data by law or to complete a pending transaction.
  • Right to opt out of marketing. You may unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the unsubscribe link in our emails, or by contacting us directly. Note that transactional and service-related communications are not subject to opt-out.
  • Right to data portability. Where technically feasible, you may request your personal data in a commonly used, machine-readable format.
  • Right to restrict processing. In certain circumstances, you may request that we limit the way we use your personal information.
  • Right to lodge a complaint. If you believe your privacy rights have been violated, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your applicable data protection authority.
