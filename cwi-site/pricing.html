Pricing questions

No meters, no gotchas.

What's the setup fee for?

It's a one-time charge so we build and connect everything for you — Starter and Growth are $499, Pro is $749 to cover the extra custom-flow build. You don't touch a thing. And if you choose annual billing, we waive the setup fee entirely.

Is there really no per-minute charge?

Correct. You pay one flat monthly rate for your plan — no per-minute meter and no surprise overage bills. You always know exactly what it costs.

How does the 30-day guarantee work?

In your first 30 days CWI will capture and book leads for you. If it doesn't recover at least one job's worth of value, that month is free — the recurring fee comes back to you. The one-time setup stays, since that covers the real build work. We take the risk so you don't have to wonder whether AI works for your customers.

Which plan should I choose?

Most CWI clients land on Growth — it actually books jobs into your calendar and handles follow-up and no-show reminders, all for about what an answering service charges to just take messages. Starter is the on-ramp if you only need leads caught after hours; Pro adds custom flows like emergency dispatch.

How much do I save with annual billing?

Annual is pay for 10 months, get 12 — so Growth works out to $331/mo instead of $397. On top of that we waive the $499 setup. That's roughly $1,293 in first-year savings on Growth versus paying monthly.

Can I switch plans later?

Anytime, up or down. Changes take effect on your next billing cycle, and annual savings carry over if you upgrade.
