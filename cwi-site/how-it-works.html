Questions

The things owners ask first.

Will callers know it's an AI?

CWI introduces itself by your business name and speaks naturally. Most callers simply feel like they reached a helpful, fast front desk. You decide how transparent to be — many businesses add “you're speaking with our virtual assistant” to the greeting.

What if it can't answer something?

You set the escalation rules. CWI can take a message, email you instantly, warm‑transfer to a team member, or schedule a callback — whatever you prefer for the questions outside its lane.

Do I have to change my phone number?

No. Keep your existing number and forward it to CWI, or we can provision a new local number. You're never locked in.

How long does setup take?

Most businesses are live the same day. Connect your number, point us at your website, confirm your services and hours, and you're answering calls within an afternoon.

Can it handle busy periods and multiple calls at once?

Yes — CWI answers every call simultaneously, so no one waits on hold and nothing rolls to voicemail, even at your busiest.
