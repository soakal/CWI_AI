Solution Overview & Demo Bridge
~5 min
▶
5:00 / 5:00
SAY

"Based on everything you've shared — the [missed calls / staffing challenge / after-hours gap] — here's where CWI AI comes in. Our AI receptionist answers every call instantly, 24/7, in your brand's voice. It can schedule, answer FAQs, qualify leads, and escalate the right calls to a real person. No hold times. No missed calls. No overhead."

TIP

Name the specific pain they gave you in Sections 2-3. Don't use generic language — mirror their words back.

SAY

"The AI learns your business — your services, pricing, hours, FAQs — and handles the repetitive stuff so your team only touches calls that actually need a human."

ASK

"Does that match what you described needing earlier, or is there a piece I should spend more time on?"

DO

Pause and let them respond. If they ask how it works technically, give a one-sentence answer and redirect to ROI: "The setup is straightforward — most businesses are live in under a week. The bigger question is what it's worth to you."

Pricing, Close & Next Steps
~4 min
▶
4:00 / 4:00
DO

Reference the ROI figure before presenting pricing: "Based on what you shared, you're likely leaving roughly $--/month on the table from missed calls alone."

SAY

"For a business at your volume, our Starter plan is the right fit — designed for teams handling up to 200 calls a month. You get 24/7 AI answering, scheduling, and FAQ handling. It's $X/month — typically less than the cost of a single missed job."

ASK

"Given what you told me about [their pain], does the Starter plan make sense as a starting point, or would you want to walk through the details side by side?"

SAY

"At your call volume, most clients land on our Growth plan — handles up to 500 calls/month with full scheduling, lead qualification, and CRM handoff. It's $X/month. Looking at the $-- in annual missed revenue you described, the ROI pays for itself fast."

ASK

"Does the Growth plan sound like it fits where you're headed, or should we talk through a custom scope?"

SAY

"Given your volume and the complexity you described, I'd recommend our Pro plan — unlimited calls, advanced routing, multi-location support, and dedicated onboarding. Pricing is $X/month. For a business your size, that's a fraction of one receptionist and covers every call."

ASK

"Would it be more helpful to set up a technical walkthrough with your team, or are you ready to look at the agreement today?"

SAY

"For an operation at your scale, we'd put together a custom Enterprise proposal — white-glove onboarding, SLA guarantees, API integration, and dedicated support. Pricing is scoped to your specific call patterns and integrations. The fastest way forward is to get your ops lead on a 30-minute scoping call with our solutions team."

ASK

"Who's the right person to pull into that scoping call — is that you, or someone on your operations side?"

SAY

"So — two options for next steps: I can send over a proposal with everything we discussed today so you can review it on your own time, or if it makes sense, we could get the paperwork started right now. Which feels right?"

TIP

Always offer a binary choice at close — never "let me know if you have questions." Silence after presenting options is their decision time. Do not fill it.
