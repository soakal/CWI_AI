2. Term and Cancellation

This Agreement is month-to-month. Either party may cancel with 30 days written notice via email. Service Provider may terminate immediately for: non-payment exceeding 14 days, unlawful use, or abusive behavior toward staff or systems. Upon cancellation, all Client data will be exported and delivered within 5 business days.

3. Payment Terms

The setup fee is due before work begins. Monthly billing commences 30 days after the go-live date and is due within 7 days of invoice. Late payment of 14 or more days may result in service suspension. An annual prepay option is available at a 10% discount (non-refundable). Price changes require 30 days written notice.

4. Intellectual Property

Service Provider retains all workflow logic, Make scenarios, system prompts, and technical configurations. Client may customize: company name and branding, colors, response tone, and FAQ content. Client owns all their own data — including customer information, conversation logs, and appointments — at all times.

5. Data & Privacy

Client data belongs exclusively to Client. Service Provider accesses data only to deliver the contracted services and will never sell or share it. All Client data will be deleted within 30 days of the cancellation effective date.

API keys are stored securely and never shared. Unique credentials are maintained per client. All administrative access is protected by two-factor authentication (2FA). Data in transit is encrypted via TLS/HTTPS. In the event of a security incident, Client will be notified within 48 hours of discovery.

P1 — System down: Response within a few hours during business hours.  P2 — Core feature broken: Same business day.  P3 — Minor issue: Within 1 business day.  P4 — Feature requests: Within 1–2 business days.
