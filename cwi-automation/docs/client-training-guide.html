2
What You Need to Do
Your 4 responsibilities
⚡
Respond to Escalations During Business Hours
When the bot flags a conversation it cannot handle, you will receive an alert in Freshchat. Respond within business hours. Escalations left unattended for more than 4 hours during business hours can result in lost leads.
📆
Keep Your Calendly Updated
The bot sends your Calendly link to every qualified lead. If your availability is outdated, leads will book times that do not work for you. Log in to Calendly at least weekly to confirm your open slots are accurate.
📬
Review Your Monthly Report Email
Your automated report arrives on the first of each month. Open it, scan the key numbers, and reply to Brian with any feedback or concerns. This is how we catch drift early and keep your system performing well.
🔑
Keep Your Payment Method Current in Stripe
Your CWI AI subscription and any third-party tool costs are billed through Stripe. A failed charge pauses automation. Log in to Stripe whenever your card changes or expires to update billing details immediately.

Quick Reference

🕐 Business Hours the Bot Knows
  • Monday – Friday: 9 AM – 6 PM local
  • Saturday: 10 AM – 3 PM (if configured)
  • Sunday: Closed by default
  • Outside hours: bot sets expectation of next-day reply
  • Holidays: contact Brian to update bot messaging
🚨 Emergency Escalation Keywords
  • Typing URGENT in Freshchat flags immediately
  • ESCALATE routes chat to your inbox
  • SPEAK TO HUMAN triggers handoff
  • MANAGER also triggers live handoff
  • Bot always offers human option after 2 failed attempts
📆 How to Update Your Availability
  • Log in at calendly.com
  • Go to Availability → Edit event type hours
  • Use "Date overrides" to block specific days
  • Changes take effect immediately for new bookings
  • Notify Brian if you change your main booking link URL
6
Monthly Review Checklist
5 things to do each month

Run through these five items at the start of each month. They take less than 15 minutes and keep your automation system healthy and effective.

1
Review Your Monthly Report Email
Open the automated report from CWI AI. Check lead volume, conversations handled, booking rate, and any flagged issues. Reply with questions or feedback.
2
Check HubSpot for Any Missed Contacts
Log in to HubSpot and filter contacts created in the past 30 days. Confirm every lead you recall speaking with appears. Flag any gaps to Brian.
3
Verify Calendly Slots Are Open for the Coming Month
Log in to Calendly and confirm you have available slots for the next 30 days. Add date overrides for vacations or known conflicts now, before leads try to book.
4
Confirm Your Stripe Subscription Is Active
Log in to your Stripe dashboard and verify your CWI AI subscription shows as Active with no failed payments. Update your payment method if your card is expiring soon.
5
Give Feedback to Brian
Send a quick note to brian@cwiai.net with anything the bot got wrong, any FAQs that came up repeatedly this month, or any new offers or pricing changes the bot needs to know about.
