When to Raise Prices — Readiness Checklist

  • 6+ months of consistent delivery
    You have reliably delivered results to clients for at least 6 consecutive months with no major service gaps.
  • 90%+ client retention rate
    At least 9 out of 10 clients are renewing each cycle, demonstrating strong satisfaction and perceived value.
  • New features or capabilities added
    You have launched new automations, integrations, reports, or service improvements since the last pricing update.
  • Operating costs have increased
    API costs, software subscriptions, team time, or infrastructure expenses have risen enough to affect margins.
  • Waitlist or demand is forming
    Prospects are waiting to onboard, or you are turning away leads — a clear market signal that prices are below demand.
Check off criteria as they apply to your current situation.

Communication Timeline

Day -30
Send initial price increase announcement email
Give clients 30 days notice. Be warm, grateful, and clear about the new pricing and effective date. Link to value delivered.
Day -14
Send friendly reminder email
Remind clients of the upcoming change. Highlight any new features or improvements added. Invite questions or a call.
Day -7
Send final reminder — one week out
Short, direct message. Confirm the new price, the effective date, and reassure grandfathered clients about their timeline.
Day 0

Update website, proposal templates, onboarding docs, and payment links. Send a brief "it's live" note to new prospects.
