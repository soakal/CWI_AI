Pipeline Structure — "AI Automation Sales"

# Stage Probability Definition Next Action
01 New Lead 5% Scraped from Google Maps, added to Apollo sequence Apollo sequence starts automatically
02 Replied 20% Responded to cold email (positive or neutral) Respond within 2 hours, send calendar link
03 Call Booked 40% Discovery call scheduled in Calendly Prep qualification notes before call
04 Proposal Sent 60% Pricing and scope delivered post-call Follow up in 3 days if no response
05 Negotiating 75% Client has questions or wants changes to scope Address all questions within 24 hours
06 Closed Won 100% Contract signed, setup fee received Begin onboarding immediately
07 Closed Lost 0% Did not close — said no or went cold Move to nurture sequence in Apollo
08 Active Client — Live, paying monthly client Monthly check-in, track support tickets
09 At Risk — Signs of churn — complaints, late payment, NPS <7 Call within 24 hours, escalate immediately
10 Churned — Cancelled the service Exit interview, update pricing/offer templates

Create Your Pipeline

Build "AI Automation Sales" with all 10 stages and deal probabilities
0 / 7
Navigate to CRM → Deals → click the pipeline dropdown → Manage Pipelines
Click "Add pipeline" → name it AI Automation Sales
Add prospect stages in order: New Lead → Replied → Call Booked → Proposal Sent → Negotiating
Add closed stages: Closed Won → Closed Lost
Add client stages: Active Client → At Risk → Churned
Set deal probability percentages on each stage (click the % field next to each stage name)
New Lead 5% · Replied 20% · Call Booked 40% · Proposal Sent 60% · Negotiating 75% · Closed Won 100% · Closed Lost 0%
Save the pipeline and confirm all stages appear in the Deals Kanban view

Monthly Client Review Process

First week of month: open all Active Client company records
Update NPS score, support ticket count, and containment rate for each client
Flag any "At Risk" clients: NPS <7, support tickets >5, or late payment
Schedule immediate call for any flagged "At Risk" clients
Log check-in call notes in HubSpot activity feed, update Last Check-In date

Weekly CRM Habits

5 minutes a day keeps your pipeline accurate and your clients healthy. These three touchpoints are all you need.

Monday
Check the pipeline — identify any deals stalled in the same stage for more than 7 days. Follow up or move to Closed Lost to keep the board clean.
Wednesday
Check the "At Risk" stage on active clients. If any client has been flagged, call them before end of day. Don't wait for churn to happen.
Friday
Log any client conversations, support interactions, or check-in calls as notes in HubSpot. Update Last Check-In date on Company records.
