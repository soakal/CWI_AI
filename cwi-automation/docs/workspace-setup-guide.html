Domain & DNS Configuration
Transfer nameservers to Netlify, then add Resend authentication records
⚠
DNS propagation takes 15–48 hours. Complete this section first and let it propagate in the background while you continue with other sections.
  • Transfer domain nameservers to Netlify DNS
    10 min
    In Netlify: Domains → Add or register domain → add your domain → copy the 4 Netlify nameservers shown.
    At your domain registrar (GoDaddy/Namecheap/etc.): replace all existing nameservers with the 4 Netlify nameservers.
  • Confirm SSL auto-provisioning in Netlify
    3 min (after propagation)
    In Netlify: Site Settings → Domain management → HTTPS → click Verify DNS configuration. Once DNS has propagated, click Provision certificate. Status should change to "Your site has HTTPS enabled."
  • Add Resend CNAME, SPF, and DKIM records
    12 min

    In Resend: Domains → Add Domain → enter your domain → Resend will show you the exact records to add. Add them in Netlify DNS (Domains → [your domain] → DNS panel).

    CNAME Resend bounce tracking subdomain
    Host:resend._domainkey.yourdomain.com Value:[shown in Resend dashboard — unique per domain] TTL:Auto / 3600
    TXT SPF record — authorizes Resend to send from your domain
    Host:@ (root domain) Value:v=spf1 include:amazonses.com ~all TTL:Auto / 3600
    TXT DKIM record — cryptographic signing key
    Host:resend._domainkey Value:p=[long public key — copy exactly from Resend dashboard] TTL:Auto / 3600
    ℹ
    After adding records, return to Resend → Domains and click Verify. All three checks (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) must show green before emails will send.
