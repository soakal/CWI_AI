1. Basic Setup
Foundation — get the core fields right first
0 / 8 ▼
Business name matches legal / DBA name exactly Avoid keyword stuffing — Google may suspend listings that add extra words.
Primary category set to core service Choose the most specific category available; add secondary categories as needed.
Phone number added and verified Use a local number when possible; toll-free is fine but local builds trust.
Website URL linked and resolving correctly Use the canonical URL with https://. Avoid redirect chains.
Service area defined (for service-area businesses) Add all zip codes or cities served. Keep the radius realistic.
Regular business hours set accurately Incorrect hours are a top complaint and can trigger negative reviews.
Holiday / special hours added for upcoming dates Set at least 90 days ahead; Google may show a "Hours may differ" warning otherwise.
Business description written (aim for 750 characters) Lead with what you do and who you serve. Include 1–2 natural keywords.
📷
2. Photos
Visual trust signals that drive clicks
0 / 6 ▼
Logo uploaded (PNG, square, 720×720 min) Your logo appears in search results and on the knowledge panel.
Cover photo uploaded (1332×750 recommended) Choose an image that represents your brand — your storefront or team in action.
Interior photos added (3+ images) Helps customers know what to expect and builds confidence before visiting.
Exterior / storefront photos added Include from multiple angles and times of day if possible.
Team photo uploaded People connect with faces — a team shot increases perceived trustworthiness.
Before-and-after or work-sample photos added Demonstrates expertise and gives prospects social proof of results.
📝
3. Posts
Keep the listing fresh and signals active
0 / 5 ▼
Weekly update post published this week GBP posts expire after 7 days — publish at least one per week to stay current.
Offer post live with coupon or promotion Offer posts include a redemption link and start/end date — great for conversions.
Event post created (if applicable) Use for workshops, open houses, webinars, or seasonal events.
Each post includes a clear call-to-action button Options: Book, Order, Learn More, Sign Up, Get Offer, Call Now.
Posts use high-quality branded images (no stock photos) Real photos outperform stock by 3–5x in click-through rate.
⭐
4. Reviews
The #1 local ranking and trust factor
0 / 6 ▼
Google review short-link generated and saved Find it in GBP dashboard under "Get more reviews." Save to CRM for reuse.
Review request sent to recent clients (last 30 days) Personalized SMS or email outperforms generic blast campaigns.
All reviews responded to (positive and negative) Respond within 24–48 hours. Mention the business name and service in replies for SEO.
Fake or spam reviews flagged for removal Use the "Report review" option. Document each flagged review with a screenshot.
Top reviews showcased on client's website Embed Google reviews widget or copy 3–5 star quotes to the testimonials page.
Average star rating is 4.0 or above Below 4.0 significantly reduces click-through. Prioritize getting new 5-star reviews.
🔧
5. Q&A and Services
Depth signals that improve local search ranking
0 / 5 ▼
Services list complete with descriptions and prices (if applicable) Each service should have a name, short description, and optional price or range.
Q&A seeded with at least 5 common questions (and answers) Post and answer questions yourself — prevents customers from leaving unanswered questions.
Products listed (if applicable) with photos and prices Use the Products tab for physical goods or packaged service offerings.
Business attributes set (accessibility, payment, amenities) Attributes like "Women-owned," "Wheelchair accessible," and "Free Wi-Fi" filter search results.
Booking button enabled and linked to scheduling system Connects to Google's booking partners or your own URL — reduces friction to convert.
