Section 1

The Call Script

Work through each phase in order. Expand a section before you enter it. Only move on when they've fully answered — silence is fine.

2 MIN PREP
Before the Call
Do this in the 2 minutes before they pick up
  • Review their industry and the outreach email they replied to
  • Note their business name, location, and estimated contact volume from Apollo
  • Have your pricing calculator open
  • Have your Calendly link ready
2 MIN
Opening
Set the frame, get them talking
"Hey [name], thanks for making time. I'll keep this to about 20 minutes — I just want to make sure I understand what's going on in your business before I say anything about what we do. Sound good?"
(They say yes)
"Perfect. So tell me — what made you respond to my email? What's going on that caught your attention?"
Listen. Don't interrupt. Let them talk.
10 MIN
Discovery Questions
Ask all 5 in order — only move on when they've fully answered
1
Volume & Current Process
"How many customer calls or messages are you getting on a typical week — and how are you handling those right now?"
2
The Pain
"What's the part of that process that's costing you the most time or causing the most headaches?"
3
Impact
"What does that actually cost you — either in time, missed jobs, or just stress?"
(Let them quantify it themselves — their number is more powerful than yours)
4
Timeline
"Is this something you're looking to fix in the next month or two, or more of a 'someday' thing right now?"
5
Decision Making
"If we put something together that makes sense, is this something you could move on, or are there other people involved in the decision?"
1 MIN
Transition
Bridge discovery to your solution
"That's really helpful — thank you. Based on what you just told me, I think I can actually help you with that. Let me show you how."
5 MIN
Presentation
Tie solution directly to their specific pain — use the industry pitch that fits
"You mentioned [their specific pain point]. Here's exactly how we solve that…"
Choose the pitch that fits their industry
Plumbers / HVAC

"We set up an AI assistant on your website that answers calls and messages 24/7 — so when someone calls at 9pm about a leak, they get an instant response and can book a time right then. You wake up with appointments already on your calendar."

Dentists

"We set up automated appointment reminders that cut no-shows by 30–50%. Patients get a text and email 48 hours before, with a one-click confirm or reschedule. Most practices recover the cost in the first month from recaptured chair time alone."

Accountants

"We automate the repetitive back-and-forth — document requests, appointment scheduling, FAQ answers — so your team spends time on actual accounting instead of inbox management. Most firms save 5–7 hours per person per week."

3 MIN
Pricing
Run the numbers, pause, let them react
"Let me run some numbers for your situation specifically."
(Open pricing calculator — plug in their numbers)
"Based on what you told me — [X contacts/week], [Y locations], [Z integrations needed] — you'd be looking at around [monthly price] per month, plus a one-time setup fee of [setup fee] to get everything built and tested."
Pause & wait
Let them respond first. Do not fill the silence.
If they ask "Is that negotiable?"
"The setup fee covers my build time — that's fixed. The monthly rate is based on your specific setup. If we simplified the scope, it could come down — but what you described sounds like the [Starter/Growth/Pro] package."
3 MIN
Close
Never end without a specific next step
If They're Interested
"Here's what I'd suggest: I'll send you a one-page summary of exactly what we'd build, the price, and the timeline. If it makes sense, we sign a simple agreement and I get started within a week. Sound fair?"
If They're Hesitant
"What's the one thing that would need to be true for this to make sense for you?"
(Listen. Address the specific objection — don't pitch more.)
If They Need to Think
"Totally fine. When specifically would you want to have a decision by?"
(Get a date. Schedule a follow-up call before you hang up.)
Always end with a specific next step
"So I'll send you the summary today, and we'll talk again on [specific date]. Does [time] work?"

Section 2

Common Objections

Use the exact responses below. Do not pitch more when they object — address the specific thing they said and move on.

Objection Response
"Too expensive" "What would make the price make sense? Let's look at what you'd save — you mentioned [their pain]. What's an hour of your time worth?"
"We're not ready yet" "Totally understand. When would be the right time? I'll follow up then — I don't want to waste your time now."
"We already tried something like this" "What happened? What didn't work?" (Listen — then address that specific failure point)
"I need to talk to my partner/wife/boss" "Makes sense. What's your take on it? What do you think they'll say?"
"Can you send me more info?" "Sure — what specifically do you want to understand better? I want to make sure I send you what's actually useful."
