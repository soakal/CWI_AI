Client Success Story
Landscaping Company — Austin, TX
How CWI AI transformed their operations with intelligent automation
The Challenge

This local landscaping company was drowning in missed calls and manual scheduling. Leads were slipping through the cracks after hours, staff spent hours each week chasing follow-ups, and their booking process required back-and-forth phone calls just to get a simple estimate on the calendar.

The Solution

CWI AI deployed a 24/7 AI receptionist that qualifies leads, answers FAQs, and books estimates directly into their calendar — all via SMS and web chat. Automated follow-up sequences were added to re-engage cold leads, and weekly performance summaries replaced manual reporting.
