Objection Responses
We already have an answering service.
Answering services take messages — CWI AI books the job. It collects the address, confirms availability, and gets a commitment from the caller while they're still in emergency mode. By the time your tech calls back, the job is already scheduled. The difference shows up on your revenue line, not your message log.
My guys handle calls just fine.
During business hours, absolutely. But 35% of plumbing calls come in after hours, and a missed burst-pipe call at 9 PM is a job that goes to whoever picks up first. CWI AI never sleeps, never misses a call, and never costs you a $350 job because someone was on another line or off the clock.
I don't think my customers will talk to a bot.
Our AI sounds like a knowledgeable dispatcher, not a phone tree. Callers don't get "press 1" — they get a natural conversation that gathers their issue, confirms urgency, and schedules service. Most callers don't realize they're not talking to a person, and the ones who do still appreciate getting an immediate, helpful response at 10 PM instead of voicemail.

Many accounting firms find that consistent after-hours availability actually smooths out seasonal peaks rather than adding to them.
