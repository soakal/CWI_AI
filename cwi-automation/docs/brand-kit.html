Minimum size

Never render the wordmark below 24px cap height. The circle mark minimum is 20px diameter. Below these sizes legibility breaks.

Clear space

Maintain clear space equal to the height of the "C" in CWI on all sides of the logo. Nothing — text, images, edges — enters this zone.

Font weight

The wordmark is always Sora 800 (ExtraBold). Never use a lighter weight for the logo. Never substitute another typeface.

Do

  • Use the approved logo files on correct backgrounds
  • Maintain proportional scaling at all times
  • Apply Signal Orange only to the "AI" or accent elements
  • Use the circle mark standalone when space is tight
  • Keep Sora 800 as the exclusive wordmark font

Don't

  • Stretch, compress, or distort the logo proportions
  • Recolor the wordmark outside the approved palette
  • Use the logo below the minimum size
  • Place the logo on busy or low-contrast backgrounds
  • Add drop shadows, outlines, or effects

04 — Voice & Tone

How We Sound

Confident and direct. We write the way a sharp person talks — no fluff, no corporate speak, no jargon. Every sentence earns its place.

⚡

Direct

Lead with the point. Cut anything that doesn't add meaning. Short sentences win.

💡

Clear

If a 10th grader can't follow it, rewrite it. Plain English always beats jargon.

💪

Confident

We don't hedge. "It works" not "it may potentially help you to possibly..."

👥

Human

We're talking to a real person with real problems. Sound like you mean it.

Words to avoid
leverage synergy robust world-class holistic cutting-edge empower ecosystem seamless innovative game-changing best-in-class scalable solutions value-add
Words we use
clear fast simple it works saves time built for you straightforward no setup fees done in minutes plain English we handle it real results
