Objection Responses

Common Objections — Pre-Written Responses

Six objections you will hear on almost every call. Copy the response, then adapt the tone to the conversation.

We already have someone who handles calls
That is great — and this is not meant to replace them. What we do is handle everything that happens when that person is unavailable: after hours, weekends, or when they are tied up on another call. So instead of those leads hitting voicemail and calling someone else, they get an instant response and can book right then. Your team still handles the relationship — we just make sure the lead does not disappear before they ever get there.
We tried chatbots before and they did not work
I hear that a lot — and honestly, most of the early chatbot tools deserved that reputation. They were rigid, gave terrible answers, and frustrated people more than they helped. What we build is different: it is trained specifically on your business, your services, and how you actually talk to customers. It knows when to answer, when to ask a clarifying question, and when to hand off to a real person. The experience feels more like a knowledgeable assistant than a bot. Would you be open to seeing a quick demo so you can judge it yourself?
I am not sure our customers would use it
That is a fair concern — and the data usually surprises people. A lot of customers actually prefer messaging over calling, especially after hours or when they are at work and cannot easily make a phone call. We typically see engagement rates in the 60–70% range within the first 30 days for local service businesses. More importantly, the people who do use it tend to book immediately rather than saying they will call back — so even a portion of your visitors using it adds up to real appointments. Would it help to see what the experience actually looks like from the customer side?
What does it cost?
Before I give you a number, let me frame it this way: if the system captures even one or two additional jobs a month that would have otherwise gone to a competitor, most clients see it pay for itself immediately. That said, our plans start at $197 per month. That includes the AI assistant, automated follow-up, and booking integration — everything set up and managed for you. There are no hidden fees and no long-term contracts. We want you to stay because it is working, not because you are locked in. Want me to walk you through exactly what is included?
We are too busy right now
That actually makes this the perfect time — when you are busy, the cost of missing a lead is even higher because you have no bandwidth to chase them down later. The whole point of this system is that it works without adding anything to your plate. Setup takes less than a week and we handle everything. Once it is live, it runs in the background. You would not even notice it is there — except in the bookings. Can we at least put a 15-minute call on the calendar for next week so you can decide with full information?
I need to talk to my partner
Absolutely — that makes total sense for a decision like this. Let me make it easy for you: I can send over a one-page summary that covers exactly what the system does, what it costs, and the results we have seen for similar businesses. That way you both have the same information going into the conversation. And if it would be helpful, I am happy to jump on a quick call with both of you together — that way neither of you has to play telephone. Which would be easier?
