Referral Program Guide

The Program

Reward
1 free month for the referring client when their referral signs up and pays their first month.
Eligibility
Any active CWI AI client can refer a business. Reward is applied to the referring client's next billing cycle.
Tracking
Brian tracks all referrals manually using the tracker in Section 3 below.
How It Works
Step 1 Client refers a local business owner to CWI AI.
→
Step 2 Referred business signs up and pays the setup fee.
→
Step 3 Referring client receives their next month free. Brian marks reward as given in the tracker.

Referral Ask Email Templates

Template B — After a positive monthly report
Subject 
Know anyone who could use results like these?
Body 
Hi [ClientName], your report this month showed [X conversations handled / X bookings automated]. If you know a colleague who could use the same results, send them my way. I offer a free month for any referral that converts.
